Good to know

A few good
questions.

Will the app send a message just because I drafted it?

Drafting and delivery are different steps. Review your message and use the authorised account workflow. Manual WhatsApp sharing still needs you to send and confirm the message.

What do I need for real calling?

A working backend, supported voice and telephony providers, credentials and a provisioned phone/SIP connection. We can walk you through the setup requirements.

Are provider calls and messages included for free?

Provider plans, quotas and usage charges vary. Ask for the current setup and costs before using paid calling or messaging.
